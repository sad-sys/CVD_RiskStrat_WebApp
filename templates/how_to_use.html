📖 Patient User Manual

1. Platform Introduction

1.1 What is this platform?

PRECISE-CVD is a research platform for cardiovascular disease risk prediction. It collects your health and lifestyle data, using advanced machine learning to help researchers better understand cardiovascular risk factors.

1.2 Purpose of the platform

This platform is for research purposes only, aiming to:

  • Assess potential risk factors for cardiovascular disease
  • Support early identification research for cardiovascular disease
  • Provide scientific evidence for future prevention strategies
⚠️ Important: This platform is for research use only. It is not for clinical diagnosis or treatment decisions. Any output should not be used as medical advice.

1.3 Who can participate?

Anyone who understands and agrees to participate can use this platform. Participation is entirely voluntary.

3. First-Time Setup

3.1 Register an Account

  1. Visit the platform homepage
  2. Click the "Sign Up" button
  3. Enter your name, email, and set a password (at least 8 characters, including letters and numbers and a special character)
  4. Read and agree to the Disclaimer and Privacy Policy
  5. Click "Register" to submit
  6. You will receive a confirmation email — click the link to activate your account

3.2 Log In

  1. Visit the platform homepage
  2. Click the "Login" button
  3. Enter your email and password
  4. Click "Login"
  5. On first login, you must accept the Terms of Use and Data Protection Notice

3.3 After Activation

  • You will be taken to your personal Dashboard
  • Once you start a new assessment, you will see three questionnaire sections:
    • Part 1: Sociodemographics
    • Part 2: Health and Medical History
    • Part 3: Sex-Specific Factors

4. Data Submission Process

Questionnaire Overview

SectionContentTimeAudience
Part 1: SociodemographicsResidence, employment, education, ethnicity, etc.5 minAll users
Part 2: Health and Medical HistoryOverall health, disease history, medications, etc.10 minAll users

Total estimated time: 15–20 minutes

4.1 Part 1: Sociodemographics

This section collects basic demographic information including residence, housing type, employment status, education level, ethnicity, and benefits received. All questions are multiple choice or numeric entry.

4.2 Part 2: Health and Medical History

This section collects your health status and medical history, including:

  • Self-rated overall health
  • Diagnosed conditions (heart disease, hypertension, diabetes, cancer, etc.)
  • Surgical history and fractures
  • Regular medications and supplements
  • Pain symptoms (headache, chest pain, back pain, etc.)

4.3 Part 3: Sex-Specific Factors

Based on your gender, the system will display different questions:

  • Male users: Facial hair growth, voice change, balding pattern, number of children fathered
  • Female users: Breast cancer screening, cervical smear, menstruation, menopause, birth history, miscarriages, contraceptive pill, HRT, ovary removal

4.4 How to Fill Out the Questionnaire

  • All questions are multiple choice (single or multiple select) or numeric entry
  • You can save your progress at any time and continue later
  • The system shows completion status for each section (Completed / In Progress / Not Started)

4.5 Submit Data

  1. Review all your entries for accuracy
  2. Click the "Submit" button
  3. Once confirmed, the system will generate your risk report
⚠️ Note: Submitted data will be used for research analysis. Please ensure all information is accurate.

5.4 What if the result is Inconclusive?

If your report shows "Inconclusive":

  • This does not mean you have or don't have a heart disease risk
  • It simply means the current data is insufficient for a clear conclusion
  • Complete all questionnaire sections for a more complete assessment
  • If still inconclusive after all sections, consult your GP or cardiologist

5.5 Important Notice

⚠️ This platform is for research purposes only. It does not provide medical advice.
  • Results should not replace your doctor's professional judgment
  • Please consult your doctor about any health concerns
  • This platform does not provide diagnosis or treatment recommendations

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do I need to pay to participate?
A: No. Participation is completely free.

Q2: How often do I need to submit data?
A: Only once at first use. If further research requires additional data, you will be notified.

Q3: Will I receive personal health advice?
A: No. This is a research tool. Please consult your doctor for any health concerns.

Q4: Will my data be shared with third parties?
A: No. Anonymised data may be used for academic publications only.

Q5: How do I withdraw my consent?
A: Contact the research team at konstantinos.theofilatos@kcl.ac.uk. Note that pseudonymised or already-analysed data cannot be withdrawn.

Q6: What if I find an error in my data?
A: Contact the research team to request a correction: konstantinos.theofilatos@kcl.ac.uk.

Q7: My result says "Inconclusive" — what does that mean?
A: It means there is not enough data for a clear conclusion. This does not mean you "have a problem" or "don't have a problem." Complete all sections, and if still inconclusive, consult your doctor.

Q8: Will the results be published?
A: Yes. Results will be published in anonymised, aggregated form in academic journals. You will not be identified.

Q9: How can I find out how my data is being used?
A: Contact the research team at any time: konstantinos.theofilatos@kcl.ac.uk.

📖 Clinician User Manual

1. Platform Introduction

1.1 What is this platform?

PRECISE-CVD is a research platform designed for clinicians and researchers to assess cardiovascular disease risk using advanced machine learning models. The platform enables both single-patient and batch risk assessment, with built-in explainability features to support research interpretation.

1.2 Purpose of the platform

This platform is for research purposes only, enabling clinicians to:

  • Assess cardiovascular risk for individual patients
  • Process multiple patient records in batch
  • Predict TAVI procedural outcomes
  • Monitor patient risk profiles over time
  • Generate explainable AI outputs for research interpretation
⚠️ Important: This platform is for research use only. It is not approved as a medical device for clinical decision-making under UK MDR 2002.

4. Batch Prediction

4.1 What is Batch Prediction?

Batch Prediction allows you to upload a CSV file containing multiple patient records and receive risk predictions for all patients simultaneously.

4.2 Accessing Batch Prediction

  1. From the Clinician Dashboard, click "Batch Prediction"
  2. You will be taken to the Batch Prediction page

4.3 Uploading Data

  1. Download the provided CSV template by clicking "Download CSV Template"
  2. Open the template in a spreadsheet application (Excel, Google Sheets, etc.)
  3. Fill in patient data following the template format:
    • Each row represents one patient
    • Each column represents a required feature
    • Ensure all required columns are populated
  4. Save the file as .csv format
  5. Click "Choose CSV file" and select your file
  6. Click "Run Prediction"

4.4 Downloading Results

  • Once processing is complete, results are displayed directly in the table
  • Results include risk scores and risk levels for each patient

6. Interpreting Risk Reports

6.1 Report Components

ComponentDescription
Risk Score0–100 value — higher indicates higher relative risk
Risk LevelHigh Risk / Inconclusive / Low Risk / Pending

SHAP Feature Importance PlotVisualisation of which features most influenced the prediction

6.2 Understanding Model Performance Metrics

MetricPlain English Explanation
AccuracyHow often the model is correct — higher is better
AUROCHow well the model separates high-risk from low-risk people — higher is better (0.5 = random, 1.0 = perfect)
C-IndexHow well the model ranks high-risk people — higher is better
PrecisionOf those labelled "high risk," how many truly are high risk
RecallOf all truly high-risk people, how many were found by the model

6.3 Interpreting SHAP Plots

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) plots show which features contributed most to a prediction:

  • Features with high SHAP values → strongly influenced the prediction
  • Features with low SHAP values → had little influence
  • Positive values → feature increased risk
  • Negative values → feature decreased risk
⚠️ Important: SHAP values are for research interpretation only. They explain model behaviour, not clinical causality.

7. Troubleshooting

"Access Restricted" Error

  • You do not have permission for that feature. Contact your administrator to request access.

No Patients Assigned

  • You have no patients assigned yet. Contact your administrator to assign patients to you.

CSV Upload Fails

  • Check that your CSV matches the template exactly. Ensure all required columns are present and correctly spelled.

Results Show "Inconclusive"

  • The model has insufficient data for a clear conclusion. Ensure all assessment steps have been completed.

  • This platform complies with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018

  • Data retention: during the study period (expected 5 years), then securely archived or deleted
  • Patients have the right to access, rectify, or withdraw consent
  • You must not share patient data outside the platform without appropriate authorisation
